About Us

弘昌環保國際有限公司（TerraSphere Recycling International Limited），紮根香港、深耕廢舊金屬回收行業多年，是一站式工業廢料回收處理商。 持有合規環保相關牌照，專門回收各類廢銅、廢鋁、不鏽鋼、廢鐵、電線電纜、機械舊料與工廠庫存廢料。 團隊具備豐富的現場清場、分類、物流經驗，堅持透明報價、現場磅重、即時結款。 我們以「變廢為寶，綠色循環」為使命，為工廠、建築業及企業客戶提供穩定可靠的廢料回收方案，在保護環境的同時為客戶創造額外收益。

TerraSphere Recycling International Limited is an experienced one-stop industrial scrap recycling service provider rooted in Hong Kong. We hold relevant environmental permits, specializing in scrap copper, aluminium, stainless steel, iron, waste cables, obsolete machinery and factory surplus materials. Our professional team provides on-site clearance, sorting and logistics services, with transparent quotation, on-site weighing and instant payment. Our mission is "Turn waste into treasure, build green circulation", delivering reliable recycling solutions for factories, construction sites and enterprises while creating extra value for our clients and protecting the environment.
